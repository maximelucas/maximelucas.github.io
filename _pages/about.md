Welcome! I am a physicist working at the intersection of dynamical systems and networks, with a strong interest in applications to the life sciences. My research combines theory, data analysis, and experiments to explore the complexity of systems such as fungal networks, brains, and animal communication.

I am an Assistant Professor ("chargé de cours") at the University of Namur and the [Namur Institute for Complex Systems](https://www.naxys.be/). I am also part of [Project CETI](https://www.projectceti.org/), a large interdisciplinary collaboration trying to decipher the communication of sperm whales.

Before that, I was an [FNRS](https://www.frs-fnrs.be/en/) postdoctoral fellow in Namur and at the [Laboratory of Mycology](https://www.uclouvain.be/en/research-institutes/eli/elim/team-declerck) (UCLouvain), and a postdoc at [CENTAI](https://centai.eu/) and the [ISI Foundation](https://isi.it) in Turin, and at [CENTURI](http://centuri-livingsystems.org/) in Marseille. I obtained my PhD in Physics from Lancaster University and the University of Florence, as part of the [COSMOS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/642563) Marie Curie network.

**Research interests**  
Complex systems · Dynamical systems · Complex networks · Synchronization · Life Sciences
